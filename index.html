Common Sorting Traps: 4 Critical Misclassifications

These common household items frequently trigger contamination alerts in municipal material recovery facilities.

TRAP #1 Pizza Box (Grease vs Clean)
INCORRECT: Blue Cart

Grease and oils saturate cellulose fibers, destroying paper recycling batch slurry.

CORRECT: Green Bin

Soiled bottom belongs in composting. Tear off unsoiled clean lid for dry paper recycling.

TRAP #2 Disposable Coffee Cups
INCORRECT: Blue Cart

Paper cups are lined with a fused polyethylene plastic film that standard hydropulpers cannot separate.

CORRECT: Landfill / Trash

Only the cardboard outer sleeve is recyclable. Plastic lid goes into blue cart; cup body goes to landfill.

TRAP #3 Thermal Cash Receipts
INCORRECT: Blue Cart

Coated with chemical Bisphenol A (BPA/BPS) dye activators that cross-contaminate recycled paper.

CORRECT: Landfill Trash

Do not compost or recycle thermal paper. Request digital e-receipts whenever possible.

TRAP #4 Lithium-Ion Batteries
INCORRECT: Regular Trash

Compactor trucks crush batteries, puncturing polymer separators and causing explosive thermal runaway.

CORRECT: Red Depot

Tape terminals with clear tape and deliver to certified battery drop-off kiosk or depot.

Knowledge Base

The Four Core Waste Streams

Separating items at the source ensures purity, prevents chemical hazards, and unlocks high-yield industrial recycling.

Green Bin

Biodegradable & Organics

Organic waste that decomposes naturally through microbial respiration, turning into nutrient-rich compost humus.

Key Items:
  • Fruit and vegetable peels & cores
  • Coffee grounds and unbleached paper tea bags
  • Garden trimmings, leaves, and cut flowers
  • Greasy pizza box bottoms & food-soiled paper napkins
Blue Cart

Dry Recyclables

Manufactured materials that can be remelted, repulped, and converted into secondary manufacturing commodities.

Key Items:
  • Plastic bottles & food containers (PET #1, HDPE #2)
  • Aluminum soda cans & clean tin soup tins
  • Clean corrugated cardboard & dry office paper
  • Rinsed glass jars and beverage bottles
Red Special Depot

Household Hazardous Waste

Toxic, flammable, corrosive, or reactive domestic items requiring strict chemical isolation and special treatment.

Key Items:
  • Lithium-ion, alkaline, and lead-acid batteries
  • Bleach, disinfectants, and harsh oven cleaners
  • Used motor engine oil, antifreeze, and brake fluids
  • Fluorescent CFL light tubes containing mercury
Slate Electronic Depot

Electronic Waste (WEEE)

Discarded electronic devices with circuit boards, microchips, and rare earth components requiring hydrometallurgical recovery.

Key Items:
  • Smartphones, tablets, laptops, and computer towers
  • Power adapters, USB cables, and appliance cords
  • Television monitors and LED displays
  • Audio equipment and domestic microwave ovens

Closed-Loop Architecture

Material Recovery Lifecycle

From residential source to secondary commodities. High-purity sorting diverts materials away from landfill leachate plumes.

STAGE 01ORIGIN

Point-of-Generation

Items are drained, rinsed, unlabelled, and placed into designated bins at home.

STAGE 02INTAKE

Sensor Sorting

Near-infrared spectrometers and eddy current magnets isolate rigid plastics, cans, and metals.

STAGE 03REFINING

Transformation

Organics undergo aerobic digestion into humus; electronic boards undergo hydrometallurgical refining.

STAGE 04OUTPUT

Circular Feedstock

High-purity recycled flakes, soil amendments, and copper ingots return to manufacturing lines.
